🏸 How the Ladder works

🏟️
4 courts · 6 players each

Every Monday, 24 players split across four courts — Court 1 is the top court.

🔄
Rotating partners

9 games per court, seed-balanced — seeds 1&2 (strongest) and 5&6 (weakest) never pair, and nobody sits out two games in a row. No cooling down!

🔢
Scoring

Every game's score is recorded — your rank is driven by win rate, volume, margin and court strength, not just turning up.

🪜
Promotions & demotions

Top 2 on each court move up, bottom 2 move down each week. Bottom 2 on Court 4 may sit out if more than 24 players are available.

🎯
Ties & re-entry

Tied on points? Head-to-head, then point difference decides. New/rejoining players start at the bottom and fight up.

📊
Season rank

Your RankScore blends win rate, volume, margin and court strength — see the leaderboard explainer above.

Full rules live in the club's shared Google Doc — ask a committee member for the link.
