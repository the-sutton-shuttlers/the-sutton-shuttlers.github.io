Privacy Policy

Sutton Shuttlers Badminton Club ("we", "the club") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact details (name, email, phone) submitted via our registration and contact forms.
  • Information you provide about playing level, availability and interests.
  • Attendance, league scores and payment records for club administration.

How we use it

  • To manage membership, sessions, leagues and tournaments.
  • To communicate club news, schedules and events.
  • To administer fees and payment tracking.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing. Data may be processed by trusted service providers (e.g. email and form services) solely to operate the club.

Your rights

You may request a copy of, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us via the contact form.

Retention

Member data is retained while you are a member and for up to 12 months afterwards, unless longer retention is required for financial records.
