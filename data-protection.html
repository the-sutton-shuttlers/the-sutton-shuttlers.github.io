Legal

Data Protection

Sutton Shuttlers Badminton Club handles personal data in line with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Data controller

The club committee acts as the data controller for member information. You can reach us via the contact form.

Lawful basis

  • Contract/legitimate interest — administering your membership, sessions, league and tournament participation.
  • Consent — where you agree to receive club communications or submit a form.
  • Legal obligation — retaining financial/payment records where required.

Security

Member data is stored in access-controlled systems used only by committee members for club administration.

Your rights

  • Access, rectification and erasure of your personal data.
  • Restriction of or objection to processing.
  • Data portability where applicable.
  • The right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
