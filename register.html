Membership

Join Sutton Shuttlers

Fill in the form below to register your interest in joining the club. A member of our committee will get back to you with session times, fees and next steps.

What happens next?

1. You'll receive a confirmation email from us.
2. We'll share session times and invite you to a taster session.
3. Once you're in, you'll get access to league play, tournaments and club events.

Already a member?

Head to the Monday Ladder page for club stats, live leaderboard and player profiles.

Questions?

Use our contact form or message us on social media.
