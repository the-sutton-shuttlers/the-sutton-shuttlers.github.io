Code of Conduct

Sutton Shuttlers Badminton Club is committed to providing a safe, friendly and inclusive environment for all members and guests. By participating in club activities, members agree to the following:

On court

  • Play fairly and respect opponents, partners, and officials at all times.
  • Accept decisions made by session organisers regarding court allocation and game formats.
  • Take care of club equipment, including nets and shuttlecocks.
  • Wear appropriate non-marking court shoes and sportswear.

Off court

  • Treat all members with respect regardless of ability, background, age, gender or identity.
  • Harassment, bullying or discriminatory behaviour of any kind will not be tolerated.
  • Represent the club positively at external fixtures and on social media.

Safety

  • Inform the committee of any injury or medical condition that may affect your participation.
  • Follow venue safety rules and instructions from session organisers.

Concerns about conduct can be raised confidentially via the contact form. The committee may suspend membership where this code is breached.
