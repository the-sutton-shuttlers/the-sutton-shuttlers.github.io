Welcome

A community built around badminton

Sutton Shuttlers is a premier badminton club based in Sutton, Surrey, dedicated to promoting the sport of badminton and fostering a vibrant community of enthusiasts. Established with a passion for the game, our club brings together players from diverse backgrounds and skill levels — from intermediate to advanced — all united by their love for badminton.

We host regular sessions two to three times a week, where members engage in dynamic doubles matches in a competitive yet friendly environment. The club ensures a seamless playing experience by providing high-quality equipment, including nets and shuttlecocks.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be an experienced player to join?
Not at all. Most of our members are intermediate to advanced players, but we welcome anyone who can sustain a rally and wants to improve. Register and we'll invite you to a taster session to see how you fit in.
When and where are sessions held?
We run sessions two to three times a week at venues in Sutton. Exact days and venues are shared with registered members each week — see the Calendar page or register to get the current schedule.
Do I need my own equipment?
Bring your own racket — the club provides nets and quality shuttlecocks. Wear non-marking court shoes; most sports halls require them.
How much does membership cost?
Fees cover court hire, shuttlecocks and events. The committee shares current session and membership fees when you register — we keep costs as low as possible as a member-run club.
Can I join the league?
Yes — any member can join the internal league. It runs weekly with a leaderboard and scheduled fixtures. Just flag it on your registration form or mention it at a session.
Is there anything besides badminton?
Plenty — the club organises hikes (like our Mount Toubkal trip), running, cricket and social events throughout the year.

Ready to get on court?

Whether you're an experienced player or looking for a fun way to stay active, register your interest and we'll be in touch.
