About Us

About Sutton Shuttlers

Welcome to Sutton Shuttlers Badminton Club, a vibrant and inclusive community of badminton enthusiasts located in Sutton, Surrey. Established in 2016, our club has grown into a hub for players of all skill levels, from intermediate to advanced, who share a passion for this fast-paced and exciting sport.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote the sport of badminton within the local community by providing a friendly, supportive, and competitive environment where players can improve their skills, enjoy the game, and connect with fellow enthusiasts. We strive to create a welcoming space that encourages sportsmanship, teamwork, and a love for the sport.

What we offer

Sessions, competition, community

Regular Sessions

We host regular badminton sessions two to three times a week, catering to players of varying abilities. Whether you're looking to improve your game, stay fit, or simply have fun, our sessions are designed to accommodate everyone.

Tournaments & Competitions

Sutton Shuttlers organizes a variety of tournaments and friendly competitions, both within the club and with other local clubs — an excellent opportunity for members to test their skills in a spirited environment.

Community & Events

Beyond badminton, we also organize other sports and recreational activities such as hiking, running, and cricket, promoting a healthy, active lifestyle for all our members.

Our Values

At Sutton Shuttlers, we believe in fostering a sense of community, promoting inclusivity, and encouraging continuous personal and athletic growth. We are more than just a badminton club; we are a community where friendships are formed, skills are honed, and passion for the game thrives.

Join Us

Whether you're an experienced player looking to compete or someone seeking a fun way to stay active, Sutton Shuttlers offers a welcoming atmosphere where you can enjoy the sport and be part of a dynamic and supportive community.
