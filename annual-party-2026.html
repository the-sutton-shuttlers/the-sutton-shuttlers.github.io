What to expect

No rackets this time — DJ, music, food, dance and full-on masti! It's your night to shine: if you'd like to perform (dance, singing, mimicry, comedy — anything fun), reach out to Deepali personally and she'll set it up.

Fee

Once registrations are in, we'll get the food quote from our caterers and announce a fee per person: free for under-5s, 50% off for ages 5–11, full price for 12 and above.
